Privacy

Short version: the tools do not send your data anywhere, because they run in your browser.

What the tools do

Images, text, files and passwords you put into a tool stay in the tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged by us. Close the tab and it is gone.

Three tools do reach out to the internet when you ask them to, and only then: the currency converter can fetch a rate, the mobile preview loads the page address you type, and the QR, GIF and PDF tools load a library from a public CDN. The tools that need a back end are marked and do not run at all.

What the site collects

Standard web server logs: IP address, browser, and which page you opened. They are kept for a short period to spot abuse and broken links.

Advertising

This site has space for ads. If you turn them on, the ad network sets its own cookies and follows its own policy — say so here and link to it before you launch.
