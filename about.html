About

Toolbench is a set of eighty small utilities. Each one does a single job, opens in under a second, and runs entirely inside your browser.

That last part is the whole point. Most tool sites upload your file to a server, convert it there and hand you a link. Here the conversion happens on your own machine using the canvas, crypto and speech APIs that every modern browser already ships. Your image never leaves the tab. Neither does the contract you pasted into the word counter.

How it is built

Plain HTML, one stylesheet, and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step for the front end, no tracking script. The header and footer are injected by assets/js/layout.js so they live in one file. The tool pages are generated from a single catalogue by build.py, which also writes sitemap.xml.

What is missing

A dozen tools in the catalogue need something a browser cannot do — a WHOIS query, a backlink index, a TLS handshake. Those pages say so plainly instead of showing a form that never works.
